**Quick preview** turns the other panel into a live preview of the file under
your cursor. Press <Shortcut id="panel.viewer.embed" /> to turn it on, and again
to turn it off.

## How it works

- Focus stays in your listing. Move the cursor and the preview follows it.
- It uses the same renderers as the [viewer](../viewer/) but picks one
  automatically. Markdown shows as a rendered document.
- In a video, <kbd>Space</kbd> in the listing plays and pauses it without leaving
  the listing.
- Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to move into the preview, for example to scroll or zoom an
  image. <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> move through its
  controls and back to the listing.
- In video and audio, <kbd>Space</kbd> plays and pauses, and <kbd>Left</kbd> /
  <kbd>Right</kbd> seek five seconds.
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> in the preview closes an expanded diagram first, then the
  preview.

Quick preview has no renderer buttons, find, word wrap or file navigation keys.
For those, choose **Open viewer** in the preview's header or press
<Shortcut id="file.view" />. The preview stays open alongside the viewer window.

## Preview what you're editing

When the active tab is an [editor tab](../editor/#editor-tabs), quick preview
shows the file you're editing and refreshes every time it saves. For Markdown it
scrolls to keep the line you're on in view, so you can write and watch the
rendered result side by side. Focus stays in the editor.
