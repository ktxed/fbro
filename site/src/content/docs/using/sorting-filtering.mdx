## Sort the listing

Press <Shortcut id="panel.sort.open" /> to open the sort selector:

- <kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Right</kbd> choose the column: **Kind**, **Name**,
  **Size** or **Date**.
- <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> switch between ascending and descending order.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> applies the sort and <kbd>Escape</kbd> cancels.

You can also click a column header in list view.

<Screenshot name="sort-selector" alt="The sort selector open over a listing, with Date chosen and descending order" />

### Remember sorting per location

By default each panel keeps its own sort order. Turn on **Remember sorting per
location** in **Settings → General** (<Shortcut id="app.settings" />) to have
fbro remember the sort order of each folder separately, in every panel and tab
and across restarts.

## Filter the listing

Press <Shortcut id="panel.filter.open" /> and start typing. The listing narrows
to matching names as you type.

- Text without `*` matches anywhere in the name: `report` finds
  `2026-report-final.pdf`.
- Text with `*` must match the whole name: `*.pdf` shows only PDFs.
- Separate alternatives with `|`: `*.jpg|*.png`.
- Matching ignores case.

While the filter field is open, <kbd>Up</kbd>, <kbd>Down</kbd> and the other
listing keys still move the cursor. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to keep the filter and
go back to the listing, or <kbd>Escape</kbd> to clear it.

The footer shows the active filter as `/pattern/`. Press
<Shortcut id="panel.filter.open" /> again to change it.

<Screenshot name="filter" alt="A listing narrowed by the filter text md, with the filter field open and /md/ shown in the footer" />

## Hidden files

<Shortcut id="panel.toggle_hidden" /> shows or hides hidden files (names
starting with a dot) in the active panel. While they're hidden, the footer
shows an **H** badge; click it to show them.

To show hidden files in every new panel, turn on **Show hidden files by
default** in **Settings → General**.
