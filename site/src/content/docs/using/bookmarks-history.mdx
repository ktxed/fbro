## Bookmarks

Press <Shortcut id="panel.bookmarks.open" />, or click **★** in the path bar,
to open the **Bookmarks** popover.

- The first entry bookmarks the current folder, or removes it if it's already
  bookmarked. The **★** is highlighted when the current folder is a bookmark.
- Move through the list with <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd>, and press
  <kbd>Enter</kbd> to go to a bookmark.
- Each bookmark has a **Remove bookmark** button.
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes the popover.

<Screenshot name="bookmarks" alt="The Bookmarks popover listing the current folder and two bookmarked folders" />

### Browse bookmarks as a listing

<Shortcut id="panel.bookmarks.browse" /> opens your bookmarks in the panel itself,
as a list you can move through with the usual keys. Open an entry to go to that
folder.

## History

Each panel remembers the folders it has visited.

- <Shortcut id="panel.history.back" /> and <Shortcut id="panel.history.forward" /> step
  back and forward.
- <Shortcut id="panel.history.open" /> opens **Navigation History**, a list of
  recent folders with the newest first. Choose one with <kbd>Up</kbd> and
  <kbd>Down</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to go there, or <kbd>Escape</kbd>
  to close the list.
