## Play media in mpv or VLC

Select videos or music files, press <Shortcut id="panel.pass_to_bro" /> and choose
**mpv** or **VLC**. fbro passes them to the player as a playlist, so selecting
hundreds of files works too. Media bros are listed first when your selection is
mostly media.

fbro finds the players here:

| Platform | Looks for |
|---|---|
| Linux | `mpv` and `vlc` on your `PATH`, plus the Flatpak apps `io.mpv.Mpv` and `org.videolan.VLC` |
| macOS | mpv on your `PATH` or as an app |
| Windows | `mpv` and `vlc` on your `PATH` and in their usual install folders |

When both a normal and a Flatpak install exist, both are listed.

## Copy Paths

**Copy Paths** copies the full paths of the selected items to the clipboard as
text, one per line. The context menu's **Copy full path** does the same for a
single item.

## Kill a process

In the [Processes](../../using/sources/#processes) source, put the cursor on a
process, press <Shortcut id="panel.pass_to_bro" /> and choose **Kill (SIGKILL)**.
The process ends immediately and gets no chance to save its work.
